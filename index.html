Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Master handles information when you use our mobile application.

Sudoku Master provides Sudoku puzzles, game progress, preferences, statistics and other gameplay features. Some gameplay information may be stored locally on your device to support these features. The information stored depends on the app's implementation and settings.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising interactions, app activity and diagnostic information, subject to the services enabled and your device settings.

For more information, please review: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps .

If in-app purchases are available in the app, transactions are processed through Google Play. Purchase information is handled according to Google's applicable policies and services.

5. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay information may be stored on your device. Data handled by third-party providers is subject to their storage, security and retention practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The app's intended audience, data practices and advertising configuration must comply with applicable laws and Google Play policies. Please contact us if you have questions about children's privacy.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can manage available advertising settings and app permissions through your device settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored gameplay information. Data retained by third-party providers may be subject to their own policies and deletion procedures.
